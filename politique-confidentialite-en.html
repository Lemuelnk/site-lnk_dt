Privacy.

This page describes the principles applied to information submitted through the website. Final contact details and legal information will be completed before the website’s final launch.

Information collected

When you use the project form, you may provide your name or your company’s name, your phone or WhatsApp number, your email address, details about your project, a preferred date, a main need, and optionally an attachment.

Why this information is used

The submitted information is used to understand your request, respond to your inquiry, prepare discussions about your project, and follow up on your request.

Client reviews

A testimonial submitted through the review form is reviewed before publication. Approved reviews may be displayed on the website with the information provided in the form, according to the studio’s publication rules.

Technical providers

The website may rely on technical services required for hosting, anti-spam protection, and form processing. Data is transmitted only as necessary for these features to function.

Your requests

For any question about the information submitted through the website, contact LNK Design Touch at contact@lnk-studio.com.
